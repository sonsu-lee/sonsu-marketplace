# Red Flags

- **"I'll offer a menu of what they might want"** — Options steer. Ask them
  to describe it first.
- **"I'll fill in sensible defaults"** — Good. Say them out loud. A default
  they never heard is one they couldn't reject.
- **"I've answered their questions, so I'll write the doc"** — Lay out the
  approaches first, in a message of their own (or, when nothing was open from
  the start, in your first message's short design).
- **"I should explain the process first"** — Ask your question. The process
  shows itself.
- **"They said 'sounds good' to the idea"** — Approval covers what you showed
  them. A design doc you haven't written isn't approved.
- **"The spike works, I'll keep building on it"** — Keeping it is a new
  request. Back through the gate.
