# Grilling

Map consequential decisions and their dependencies as a design tree. Research
facts in the environment first; do not ask the user for facts you can look up.
For each round, identify the frontier: important decisions whose prerequisites
are settled. Ask independent frontier questions together, with a recommended
answer and its reason. Leave dependent questions for later rounds. Recompute
as answers and evidence arrive. Do not repeat information already provided.

Stop when the consequential uncertainties are resolved. Confirm shared
understanding where necessary; an interview alone does not authorize writes,
implementation, publication, or any other side effect. User and project
instructions take precedence over this workflow.

## Evaluate the interview

Assess purpose and scope, evidence, substantive alternatives, decided versus
open questions, and verifiability. Give each applicable item 0, 1, or 2;
normalize the total to 10 and record exclusions and reasons. Require 8/10 and
verified requirements, authorization, security and data safety, confirmed
contracts, and appropriate evidence. Fix supported failures and reevaluate up
to three times; do not repeat without changes. If still unsuccessful, stop and
report each score, mandatory conditions, evidence, and blockers. Wait for a
user decision, permission, or access rather than automatically retrying it.
