# State the decision in a sentence

- Date: YYYY-MM-DD
- Status: proposed

## Context

Explain the problem or constraint requiring a decision. Link supporting issues,
documentation, and measurements instead of repeating them.

## Decision

State what is chosen, where it applies, and why, in a few sentences.

## Rejected alternatives

- Describe an alternative actually considered and why it was not chosen.

## Consequences

Explain the benefits, costs, risks, and options this choice rules out. Note
uncertainty or conditions for reconsideration when they matter.
