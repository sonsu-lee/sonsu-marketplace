# Conditional graphic craft and handoff

Use these sections for the corresponding construction or delivery decision.
Source passages and sampled frames support contextual advice, not universal
recipes, measured outcomes, production approval or a performed workflow on the
current asset. Keep the source's observation separate from current verification.

## Icons and rendering

Judge the family together: silhouette, visual weight, corners and
recognizability at the intended size. Do not force every tiny detail to remain
outlined when it collapses. P07 context at
[363.58–387.029 seconds](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=i8jG_5iKELI&t=363s)
includes a 372-second sample with a tiny solid mark in an otherwise outlined
train icon; the 294-second sample supplies the family comparison.

Keep export bounds separate from optical construction. Preserve proportions, and
distinguish a path's bounds from the bounds contributed by its stroke. Do not
derive a universal pixel grid from a particular editor demonstration. P34
context:
[96.479–134.93 seconds](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Vdp1zUqmYQc&t=96s) and
[391.56–417.83 seconds](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Vdp1zUqmYQc&t=391s),
with the continuation at
[419.4–449.51 seconds](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Vdp1zUqmYQc&t=419s).

Inspect the exported result in the actual renderer and scale, not just the
vector canvas. Simplify details that disappear while retaining meaningful
differences. P35–P36 context:
[254.76–299.03 seconds](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Vdp1zUqmYQc&t=254s) and
[630.42–659.99 seconds](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Vdp1zUqmYQc&t=630s).
Neither the demonstration nor a local preview establishes usability for users or
compatibility with every renderer.

## Graphic meaning

When a graphic looks like data, make its intended mapping interpretable and
avoid implying magnitude or order that the content does not support. P45 source
context includes
[898.88–949.309 seconds](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=-Jzn4PnTmoU&t=898s) and
[259.06–320.19 seconds](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=65lKaReFEYA&t=259s).
These are a creator's interpretations of static examples, not proof that the
underlying data is wrong or that every viewer will misread it.

## Preserve useful editable states

Before expansion, merging or another destructive authoring step, retain a
representation that is useful for anticipated editing, comparison or return.
This can be a meaningful stage, original group, live construction or version; it
is not a rule to duplicate every layer. Expansion and merging remain valid when
appropriate. Do not infer saved history or editability from appearance.

W05 context:
[244.32–328.76 seconds](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=YWZGsn0T-sE&t=244s). The
309-second frame shows motif copies and selected path handles during the
live-shape explanation; captions provide the warning about premature expansion.
A still does not reveal every object's internal state. No recovery success, time
saving or improved quality was independently measured.

## Reproduction and delivery

When a real supplier or medium requires a monochrome treatment, consider the
complete identity, including symbol and wordmark, and retain the full-color
original. Do not prescribe a universal variant inventory or prohibit gradients
across all signage and print. Do not apply this advice to informative UI text or
icons merely because they use color.

W06 context:
[213.7–254.66 seconds](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5HEoiIVjX74&t=213s). The
228-second sample is an existing gradient identity presentation, not a completed
monochrome revision or manufacturing test. State which output, constraints and
production checks remain unverified.

When the agreed handoff includes an editable master and a corresponding
presentation, keep the master consistent with the treatment that presentation
shows for the delivery. If either artifact is unavailable, leave their
consistency unassessed. W01 context is
[581.44–601.829 seconds](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=uJy-r9cgphg&t=581s).
This is the speaker's caution, not evidence that a correct editable deliverable,
client approval or calibrated output was inspected. Describe the representation
actually delivered; a preview is not the source file or a production sign-off.
